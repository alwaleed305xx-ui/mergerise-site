2. The game

MergeRise is a single-player merge puzzle and dress-up story. It can be downloaded and played without paying; optional in-app purchases are available. Rivals in the game are computer-controlled AI rivals: there is no multiplayer, chat or user-generated content. An internet connection is needed only for purchases.

4. Purchases

Purchases in the iPhone app are made through the App Store with Apple's In-App Purchase, under Apple's terms. The price the App Store shows (in your local currency, including applicable taxes) is the price you pay. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policy (reportaproblem.apple.com); if Apple refunds a purchase, the items it delivered may be removed.

  • Gem packs (100 to 15,000 gems): the stated gems, bonus included, are added at once.
  • 30-Day Pass: a one-time purchase that does not renew automatically and is not a subscription. For 30 days it pays 150 coins and 10 gems per day when you open the game (missed days are paid the next time you open it). It delivers coins and gems only. Buying it again adds 30 days.
  • Starter Pack: can be bought once. It gives 3,000 coins, 400 gems and the exclusive Gold Jersey.
  • Break the Piggy: offered when the bonus piggy bank holds at least 240 gems earned by winning stages; it adds all the gems saved in the piggy.

Restore Purchases (in Settings and at the bottom of the Shop) restores the Starter Pack's Gold Jersey and its owned status, but not its coins and gems. Gem packs, the 30-Day Pass and Break the Piggy are consumable: they are used up when delivered and are not restored on a new device or after the app is deleted.

5. Random items (Premium Box)

The Premium Box costs 36 gems and gives one clothing item chosen at random from the items you do not own yet, at the odds shown on the button and on the confirmation before you buy (Epic 85% · Legendary 15%; when only one rarity is left, it is 100% that rarity). The worst outcome is an epic item, which costs the same 36 gems to buy directly. Nothing is wagered, and nothing can be cashed out.
