Privacy Policy

Developer: MergeRise · Last updated: 2026-10-15 · Applies to the MergeRise apps for iPhone and Android (Google Play) and the MergeRise web demo.

In short
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect any personal data. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

MergeRise ("we") is the developer of the MergeRise game, a single-player merge puzzle and fashion story for iPhone and Android. For questions, see Contact (section 12).

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data, and it contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software. We do not run a server that receives data from the game, and we do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

3. Data stored on your device

To keep your game, the app saves your progress on your device: in its local storage (localStorage, key mergerise.save.v1) and, in the iPhone and Android apps, also in the app's own storage on the device, so that a cleared web cache does not lose it. The save holds the hero name you type, gender and skin tone, stages and stars, coins and gems, the clothes you own and wear, a record of your purchases (product, transaction ID or purchase token, and date, so every item is delivered once and can be restored) and settings such as sound and language.

This data stays on your device and is never sent to us, so we cannot see, recover or move it. Deleting the app deletes it from your device. Your device's own backup may include it, depending on your settings: on iPhone, iCloud Backup, which Apple runs for you; on Android, Android backup, which Google runs and which you switch on or off in your phone's settings. If such a backup includes MergeRise, your progress can come back when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone with the same account. We never receive these backups. You can also erase earned progress with Settings → Reset progress; purchased items are kept (see the Terms of Use).

4. In-app purchases on iPhone (Apple)

Purchases in the iPhone app use Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple takes the payment and handles your Apple Account and payment details; we never receive your name, email address, card or bank details. To deliver what you bought, the app receives from Apple only the product, a transaction ID and the purchase date, and keeps those IDs on your device so that nothing is delivered twice. They are not sent to us. We do not use any third-party purchase, receipt or subscription service. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. In-app purchases on Android (Google Play)

Purchases in the Android app use Google Play's billing system. Google takes the payment and handles your Google account and payment details; we never receive your name, email address, card or bank details. To deliver what you bought, the app receives from Google Play only the product, a purchase token and the purchase date, and keeps them on your device so that nothing is delivered twice. They are not sent to us. We do not use any third-party purchase, receipt or subscription service. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Advertising

The iPhone and Android apps show no ads and contain no advertising SDK. They do not use your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iPhone, the advertising ID on Android: the Android app does not even request the permission for it), and the iPhone app does not ask for permission to track you. The web demo only shows a pretend "demo ad" card drawn by the game itself; nothing is loaded from an ad network. If we ever add ads to a future version, we will update this policy, and the privacy details on the App Store and the Data safety section on Google Play, before that version is released.

7. Analytics and network use

There are no analytics. The app makes no network requests of its own: its only connection is to Apple (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android) when you view prices, buy or restore purchases. Links to this page, the Terms of Use or the support page open in your device's web browser, outside the game.

8. Fonts

The fonts (Poppins, Cairo and Baloo 2, under the SIL Open Font License) are bundled inside the app and the web demo. Nothing is loaded from Google Fonts or any other font service.

9. Web demo and support website

If you play the web demo in a browser, it saves your progress in the browser's local storage and keeps an offline copy of the game's own files (a service worker). Its purchase buttons are a demonstration only: no payment is taken and nothing is sent anywhere.

Our support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). If you contact us through GitHub Issues, your GitHub username and what you write are public and handled under GitHub's terms, so please do not post personal information there. If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and delete them when the matter is closed.

10. Children

MergeRise is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we collect none from children either. Purchases always need the approval of the store account holder: on iPhone, parents can control them with Screen Time and Ask to Buy; on Android, with Google Family Link or the Play Store setting that asks for authentication on every purchase.

11. Your rights (PDPL and GDPR)

If you live in Saudi Arabia, the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) gives you rights over your personal data, including the rights to be informed, to access, to correct and to request deletion. If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the GDPR / UK GDPR gives you similar rights, including the right to object.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete: your game data is only on your device, and you can delete it at any time by deleting the app (and, if you use one, its copy in your device's backup). Purchase records are held by Apple (privacy.apple.com) or by Google (policies.google.com/privacy). You can still send us any privacy question or request (section 12), and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Saudi Arabia, the Saudi Data and AI Authority, SDAIA).

12. Contact

Support page: alwaleed305xx-ui.github.io/mergerise-site
Privacy questions and requests: email the developer address shown under "App support" on the MergeRise page on Google Play (no GitHub account needed), or open an issue on GitHub. Please do not include personal information in a public issue.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app would collect any data, we will update this policy, the App Store privacy details and the Google Play Data safety section before that version is released.
