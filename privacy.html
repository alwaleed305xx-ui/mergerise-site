Privacy Policy

Developer: MergeRise · Last updated: 2026-10-15 · Applies to the MergeRise app for iPhone and the MergeRise web demo.

In short
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect any personal data. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

MergeRise ("we") is the developer of the MergeRise game, a single-player merge puzzle and dress-up story. For questions, see Contact (section 11).

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data, and it contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software. We do not run a server that receives data from the game, and we do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

3. Data stored on your device

To keep your game, the app saves your progress in its local storage on your device (localStorage, key mergerise.save.v1): the hero name you type, gender and skin tone, stages and stars, coins and gems, the clothes you own and wear, a record of your purchases (so every item is delivered once and can be restored) and settings such as sound and language.

This data stays on your device and is never sent to us, so we cannot see, recover or move it. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup, which Apple runs for you) may include it, depending on your device settings. You can also erase earned progress with Settings → Reset progress; purchased items are kept (see the Terms of Use).

4. In-app purchases (Apple)

Purchases in the iPhone app use Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple takes the payment and handles your Apple Account and payment details; we never receive your name, email address, card or bank details. To deliver what you bought, the app receives from Apple only the product, a transaction ID and the purchase date, and keeps those IDs on your device so that nothing is delivered twice. They are not sent to us. We do not use any third-party purchase, receipt or subscription service. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Advertising

The iPhone app shows no ads, contains no advertising SDK, does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for permission to track you. The web demo only shows a pretend "demo ad" card drawn by the game itself; nothing is loaded from an ad network. If we ever add ads to a future version, we will update this policy (and the App Store privacy details) before that version is released.

6. Analytics and network use

There are no analytics. The app makes no network requests of its own: its only connection is to Apple when you view prices, buy or restore purchases. Links to this page, the Terms of Use or the support page open in Safari.

7. Fonts

The fonts (Poppins, Cairo and Baloo 2, under the SIL Open Font License) are bundled inside the app and the web demo. Nothing is loaded from Google Fonts or any other font service.

8. Web demo and support website

If you play the web demo in a browser, it saves your progress in the browser's local storage and keeps an offline copy of the game's own files (a service worker). Its purchase buttons are a demonstration only: no payment is taken and nothing is sent anywhere.

Our support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). If you contact us through GitHub Issues, your GitHub username and what you write are public and handled under GitHub's terms, so please do not post personal information there.

9. Children

MergeRise is a general-audience game (App Store age rating 9+) and is not directed specifically at children. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we collect none from children. Purchases always need the approval of the Apple Account holder, and parents can control them with Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

10. Your rights (PDPL and GDPR)

If you live in Saudi Arabia, the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) gives you rights over your personal data, including the rights to be informed, to access, to correct and to request deletion. If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the GDPR / UK GDPR gives you similar rights, including the right to object.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete: your game data is only on your device, and you can delete it at any time by deleting the app. Purchase records are held by Apple (privacy.apple.com). You can still send us any privacy question or request (section 11), and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Saudi Arabia, the Saudi Data and AI Authority, SDAIA).

11. Contact

Support page: alwaleed305xx-ui.github.io/mergerise-site
Contact us here: open an issue on GitHub. Please do not include personal information in a public issue.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app would collect any data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
